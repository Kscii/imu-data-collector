# IMU-0002-R01 BLE protocol audit — 2026-09-08

This document separates host observations, supplier claims, and hypotheses. It is the
pre-implementation evidence record for the `acce&gyro` sensor assigned the logical serial
number `IMU-0002-R01`.

## ABF2 notification contract

Every one of 1,007 notifications captured during the 20 second audit was exactly 22 bytes and
contained exactly one sensor sample:

```text
offset  size  type                    meaning
0       2     little-endian int16     raw acceleration X
2       2     little-endian int16     raw acceleration Y
4       2     little-endian int16     raw acceleration Z
6       2     little-endian int16     raw angular velocity X
8       2     little-endian int16     raw angular velocity Y
10      2     little-endian int16     raw angular velocity Z
12      8     little-endian uint64    device-local monotonic milliseconds
20      2     bytes                   fixed CR LF suffix: 0D 0A
```

Example notification:

```text
45 FB 8D FE A1 41 FB FF 03 00 01 00 56 B7 08 00 00 00 00 00 0D 0A
```

It decodes to six-axis counts `[-1211, -371, 16801, -5, 3, 1]` and device time
`571222 ms`.

The device-time difference was 20 ms for 1,005 intervals and 30 ms for one interval. The
device-clock span rate was about 49.98 Hz; the host callback span rate was about 50.01 Hz.
Host callbacks arrived in BLE scheduling bursts (median callback separation about 0.69 ms), so
host callback intervals must not be used as the sample timeline when the device counter exists.

After disconnecting and reconnecting, the counter continued from roughly `591352 ms` to
`1212336 ms`. It is not Unix/world time and does not reset on an ordinary BLE reconnect. Whether
it resets after power loss remains unverified.

## Stationary observation and conversion status

The sensor was stationary on a table. Across 1,007 samples, little-endian channel statistics
were approximately:

| Channel | Mean counts | Population standard deviation |
|---|---:|---:|
| acceleration X | -1215.13 | 16.25 |
| acceleration Y | -367.19 | 18.54 |
| acceleration Z | 16820.41 | 20.97 |
| angular velocity X | -5.77 | 0.94 |
| angular velocity Y | 3.14 | 1.36 |
| angular velocity Z | 2.47 | 0.73 |

The mean acceleration-vector magnitude is about `16868 counts`. A nominal
`16384 counts/g` hypothesis would produce approximately `10.10 m/s^2`; the old CW12EU-T
`4096 counts/g` conversion would produce approximately `40.39 m/s^2` and is incompatible with
this observation. `16384 counts/g` is diagnostic-only until six-face characterization is
complete. Gyroscope scale, axis mapping, exact bias, range, clock reset behaviour, long-run
reliability, and `ABF4` semantics remain unverified.

The supplied 16-byte big-endian acceleration/gyroscope/pressure description does not describe
the observed stock ABF2 stream. The raw notification bytes must always be retained so that any
later protocol correction can be applied without modifying the evidence capture.

## Post-implementation acceptance

A second read-only check exercised the implemented `IMU-0002-R01` profile against the physical
device. A five-second `probe-imu` run received and parsed 251 of 251 notifications, all 22 bytes,
with no parser error or callback drop. The host-span estimate was `50.251 Hz`; the device-clock
span estimate was `49.900 Hz`, with no counter reset.

An independent five-second IMU-only `test` capture then wrote capture HDF5 schema `1.8.0` and
passed validation with no issue or warning. It contained 251 raw packets/samples, retained 5,522
payload bytes, froze SN `IMU-0002-R01`, profile SHA-256
`fcefe1a31775a291a86c38bad35919549023bdd387e62b506537973a6ad1e32c`, and protocol
`acce_gyro_abf0_v1`. The measured mean counts were approximately
`[-1171.52, -322.83, 16766.90, -6.02, 3.44, 2.67]`; acceleration-vector magnitude was
`16810.88 counts`. All `values_si` entries were NaN as required for an unverified conversion.
The acceptance capture was isolated under `/tmp`, was not uploaded, and did not write any BLE
characteristic.

## 2026-09-16 production SI decision — reviewed family exception

This section supersedes the earlier statement that `16384 counts/g` is only a diagnostic
hypothesis. It does not turn the experiment into a laboratory-traceable or per-device
calibration.

The production candidate uses the nominal sensitivities in the official Bosch BMI160
datasheet for the configured ranges:

- `16384 counts/g` at `±2 g`;
- `16.4 counts/(°/s)` at `±2000 °/s`;
- zero external accelerometer and gyroscope bias compensation;
- raw axis order `[0, 1, 2]` and runtime axis signs `[1, 1, 1]`.

Official source: [BMI160 Datasheet](https://www.bosch-sensortec.com/media/boschsensortec/downloads/datasheets/bst-bmi160-ds000.pdf).
The values are manufacturer-typical sensitivities, not guarantees that this individual unit has
zero residual offset. The zero bias arrays therefore mean “no external offset compensation”, not
“measured offset is zero”.

The operator's experiment report is preserved byte-for-byte as
[`evidence/IMU-0002-R01-cal-c43dacc6791746219c38c498d5710878-original.json`](evidence/IMU-0002-R01-cal-c43dacc6791746219c38c498d5710878-original.json),
SHA-256 `995357669a77cde2a5b59c2e21a7a7179c0e8a237bba6f4d25480ebe2b986fa6`.
During all retained gyroscope trials, the selected signed face pointed upward and the board was
rotated clockwise as viewed from above. By the right-hand rule, that motion is opposite the
reference sign entered in the experiment UI. The auditable derivative
[`evidence/IMU-0002-R01-cal-c43dacc6791746219c38c498d5710878-direction-corrected.json`](evidence/IMU-0002-R01-cal-c43dacc6791746219c38c498d5710878-direction-corrected.json)
therefore negates every gyroscope trial's `sign` and `reference_angle_deg`, and negates the 31
stored fit estimates. It does not change the runtime axis mapping. Its SHA-256 is
`6bd33daeff85d3fb7a44c6c950014c4b322e2a7430e4504d75cede264c043420`.

The corrected fit estimates have median `16.401`, mean `16.4080`, population standard deviation
`0.1503`, and range `16.0662–16.7122 counts/(°/s)`. The six-face aggregate acceleration estimate
was `16366 counts/g`, about `0.11%` below the manufacturer-typical value. These observations are
supporting plausibility checks; the production coefficients come from the datasheet.

The test board was taped to a power bank. Tilt, repeated placement, tape pressure, PCB bending,
and hand rotation can introduce systematic error, so the experiment's fitted biases are not used
in production. The nine source HDF5 files referenced by the report are unavailable to the current
reviewer. Consequently, the corrected derivative preserves their recorded hashes but is not
compatible with the normal calibration-experiment upload contract, and the 12 gyroscope
validation integrations cannot be recomputed. The derivative remains `test`,
`training_eligible=false`, and unverified.

Allowing this SI profile for `prod` is an explicit administrator-reviewed family exception based
on the manufacturer nominal sensitivities, the observed protocol/rate, and the representative
experiment. It is not a claim of individual metrological calibration.
